Implementation Roadmap

A phased approach to implementing sustainable improvements in student progression

Roadmap Overview

Implementation is structured in phases across an 18-month period, allowing for proper planning, resource allocation, and adaptation based on early results.

Phase 1: Foundation & Planning (Months 1-3)

🎯 Objectives

  • Establish implementation team
  • Secure stakeholder commitment
  • Detailed planning of interventions
  • Resource allocation

📋 Key Activities

  • Kick-off meetings with leadership
  • Staff training sessions
  • Baseline data collection
  • Infrastructure preparation

📊 Success Indicators

  • Team established and trained
  • Budget allocated
  • Baseline metrics recorded
  • Staff engagement documented

Phase 2: Pilot & Learning (Months 4-9)

🎯 Objectives

  • Pilot interventions with select programmes
  • Gather implementation feedback
  • Assess early impact
  • Refine approaches

📋 Key Activities

  • Launch Early Alert System (pilot)
  • Begin Peer Mentoring Programme
  • Start Skills Workshops
  • Establish data monitoring

📊 Success Indicators

  • Participation rates
  • Early engagement metrics
  • Stakeholder feedback positive
  • No major implementation barriers

Phase 3: Scale & Expand (Months 10-15)

🎯 Objectives

  • Expand successful pilots
  • Launch programme-level interventions
  • Establish cross-departmental coordination
  • Integrate data systems

📋 Key Activities

  • Roll out Early Alert System broadly
  • Expand mentoring to all programmes
  • Begin curriculum redesign work
  • Launch data dashboards

📊 Success Indicators

  • Institution-wide participation
  • Early impact on progression metrics
  • Strong cross-departmental engagement
  • Data systems operational

Phase 4: Consolidation & Review (Months 16-18)

🎯 Objectives

  • Comprehensive impact evaluation
  • Consolidate successful practices
  • Plan for sustainability
  • Prepare for next cycle

📋 Key Activities

  • Final data collection
  • Impact analysis and reporting
  • Stakeholder review meetings
  • Institutionalisation planning

📊 Success Indicators

  • Measurable progression improvements
  • Sustained staff engagement
  • Student satisfaction improved
  • Systems embedded in practice

Key Milestones

Month 3

Foundation phase complete, all teams trained and ready

Month 6

Pilot programmes showing positive results

Month 12

Institution-wide implementation achieved

Month 18

Comprehensive evaluation and next-cycle planning

Success Metrics

Progress will be tracked against the following institutional metrics:

Progression Outcomes

  • Student completion rates within standard programme duration
  • Progression from year to year across all programmes
  • Attrition rates by programme and student group

Support Effectiveness

  • Support service utilization rates
  • Student satisfaction with support services
  • Early intervention uptake and effectiveness

Institutional Capacity

  • Staff awareness and engagement
  • Data system functionality and utilization
  • Cross-departmental collaboration indicators

Student Experience

  • Sense of belonging and engagement
  • Academic confidence and self-efficacy
  • Overall satisfaction with programmes
