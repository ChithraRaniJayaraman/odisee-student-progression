Problem Analysis

Student progression challenges are not isolated events. They emerge from interacting academic, social, organisational, and policy-level conditions.

Key Challenges

Transition Gaps

Students entering higher education often face misalignment between prior preparation and programme expectations.

Fragmented Support

Services exist but are not always coordinated or visible early enough for at-risk students.

Data Delays

Warning signals are frequently identified late, reducing the effectiveness of interventions.

Research Questions

  1. Which factors most strongly influence progression patterns across programmes?
  2. How do those factors interact over time to reinforce success or risk?
  3. What leverage points can improve outcomes with sustainable impact?
