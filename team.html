Research Approach

Our research combines:

  • Qualitative Analysis: Stakeholder interviews, focus groups, and institutional observations
  • Quantitative Data: Progression metrics, retention statistics, and outcome measures
  • Systems Mapping: Causal loop diagrams and systems models
  • Action Research: Participatory intervention design and implementation

Institutional Affiliation

Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB)

Faculty of Social Sciences and Human-IT

This research is conducted as an MSc thesis in Educational Sciences. VUB is committed to evidence-based approaches to improving educational practice and institutional effectiveness.

Partner Institution

Odisee University of Applied Sciences

As the focus institution for this research, Odisee provides access to data, stakeholder engagement, and the opportunity to implement evidence-based interventions to enhance student progression.

This partnership exemplifies the value of academic-practitioner collaboration in addressing real-world challenges in higher education.

Collaboration & Engagement

We believe in participatory research where stakeholders are partners, not just subjects. Throughout this project, we:

Engage Stakeholders

  • Regular feedback sessions with Odisee staff and leadership
  • Student voice in problem diagnosis and solution design
  • Transparent communication of findings and implications

Build Capacity

  • Staff training in systems thinking and data-informed practice
  • Knowledge transfer to support sustainability
  • Documentation and resources for ongoing implementation

Share Knowledge

  • Academic publications in peer-reviewed journals
  • Conference presentations and workshops
  • Practitioner-focused resources and case studies
