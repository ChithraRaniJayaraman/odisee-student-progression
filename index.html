Welcome to Our Research

This thesis project applies systems-thinking principles to improve programme-level academic success at Odisee. By examining the interconnected factors affecting student progression, we develop evidence-based interventions that support student achievements and programme quality.

🎯 Our Focus

Understanding the complex relationships between student characteristics, institutional factors, and academic outcomes through a systems lens.

🔍 Our Approach

Data-driven analysis combined with qualitative insights to identify leverage points for effective intervention and sustainable change.

📈 Our Goal

Enhance student progression rates and institutional learning while maintaining educational quality and inclusivity at Odisee.
