MSc Educational Sciences Thesis | VUB

A Systems-Thinking Approach to Improving Programme-Level Academic Success at Odisee

This research examines how academic design, support structures, institutional coordination, and student experience interact over time. The goal is to identify high-leverage interventions that strengthen progression and reduce attrition.

Project Snapshot

Research Context

MSc thesis at Vrije Universiteit Brussel focused on programme-level student success in applied higher education.

Core Method

Systems thinking with qualitative inquiry, progression data analysis, and participatory intervention design.

Expected Outcome

A practical, evidence-informed roadmap that Odisee can sustain through coordinated institutional practice.

Research Questions

Q1

What are the main factors influencing student progression at programme level?

Q2

How do these factors interact to produce reinforcing success or risk patterns?

Q3

Which intervention portfolio can improve progression outcomes with institutional feasibility?

Implementation Phases

Months 1-3

Foundation & Planning

Governance setup, baseline data capture, and team preparation.

Months 4-9

Pilot & Learning

Small-scale implementation with rapid feedback loops.

Months 10-15

Scale & Expand

Broader rollout of validated interventions and coordination routines.

Months 16-18

Consolidation & Review

Impact evaluation, institutional embedding, and next-cycle planning.
