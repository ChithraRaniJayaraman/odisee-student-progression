======= Peer Mentoring Toolkit

Peer Mentoring Toolkit

Pilot-ready guidance for first-year belonging, low-threshold peer support and referral within Odisee Brussels' first-year support pathway.

Toolkit at a Glance

Peer connection > Check-in > Support conversation > One small next step > Navigation or referral > Follow-up

Purpose

1. Strengthen first-year students' sense of belonging through low-threshold peer contact. 2. Structure Odisee's existing buddy or peer-support practices without replacing them. 3. Help peer mentors guide students toward appropriate support when ordinary peer support is not enough.

Positioning Note

This toolkit translates the peer mentoring deliverable into a pilot-ready, low-burden tool for Odisee Brussels. It helps second-year or senior students support first-year students through approachable check-ins, role clarity and referral guidance. It does not replace existing buddy systems or formal student support.

Suggested Check-In Structure (Five Steps)

  1. Welcome and personal check-in — "How have the last few weeks been for you?"
  2. What is coming up? — "What is coming up soon that feels important or difficult?"
  3. Main concern or question — "Is there one thing you would like to figure out today?"
  4. One small next step — "What is one small step you could take this week?"
  5. Confirm contact or support route — "Would it help to check in again, or should we look at who you could contact?"
