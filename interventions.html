Intervention Portfolio

Interventions are designed as a connected portfolio, so improvements in one area reinforce gains in others.

Student-Level Interventions

Early Alert Support

Detect risk indicators quickly and coordinate outreach before difficulties intensify.

Peer Mentoring

Support belonging, practical guidance, and motivation through structured peer networks.

Skills Development

Provide targeted workshops on study planning, self-regulation, and assessment readiness.

Programme-Level Interventions

Curriculum Coherence

Align learning outcomes, teaching activities, and assessment to reduce confusion and overload.

Assessment Redesign

Use diverse, staged assessments with actionable feedback and transparent criteria.

Integrated Support Pathways

Make student support visible inside the programme journey, not separate from it.

Institutional-Level Interventions

Data-Informed Decision Structures

Use shared dashboards and regular review cycles to steer implementation.

Cross-Unit Coordination

Define roles and handoffs across academic, student support, and leadership teams.

Capacity Building

Develop staff confidence in systems thinking, intervention design, and improvement routines.
