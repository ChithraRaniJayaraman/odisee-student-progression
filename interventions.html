Proposed Interventions

Evidence-based strategies designed to improve student progression outcomes

Intervention Framework

Our proposed interventions are grounded in the systems model and organised across three levels: student support, programme enhancement, and institutional coordination.

Student-Level Interventions

1. Early Alert System

Objective: Identify at-risk students early and provide timely support

Key Elements:

  • Data-driven identification of risk factors
  • Proactive outreach during first semester
  • Personalised support plans
  • Regular progress monitoring

Expected Outcomes: Reduced attrition, improved progression rates

2. Peer Mentoring Programme

Objective: Leverage peer support for student success

Key Elements:

  • Structured peer mentoring relationships
  • Mentor training and support
  • Integration with academic programmes
  • Focus on critical transition points

Expected Outcomes: Improved sense of belonging, increased engagement

3. Skills Development Workshops

Objective: Build academic and professional skills

Key Elements:

  • Study skills and time management
  • Critical thinking and research methods
  • Digital literacy and tools
  • Wellbeing and resilience building

Expected Outcomes: Improved academic performance, increased confidence

Programme-Level Interventions

1. Curriculum Redesign

Objective: Enhance curricula for accessibility and relevance

Key Elements:

  • Inclusivity audit of current curricula
  • Alignment with contemporary industry standards
  • Integration of practical application opportunities
  • Flexible learning pathways

Expected Outcomes: Higher engagement, improved learning outcomes

2. Enhanced Assessment Practices

Objective: Improve feedback quality and alignment

Key Elements:

  • Formative assessment strategies
  • Timely and constructive feedback
  • Assessment diversity to accommodate learning styles
  • Clear progression criteria and expectations

Expected Outcomes: Clearer learning pathways, reduced assessment anxiety

3. Integrated Support Model

Objective: Coordinate academic and pastoral support

Key Elements:

  • Integration of support services within programmes
  • Staff training in holistic student support
  • Clear referral pathways and protocols
  • Regular coordination meetings

Expected Outcomes: Seamless support experience, better resource utilization

Institutional-Level Interventions

1. Data-Informed Decision Making

Objective: Build institutional capacity for evidence-based planning

Key Elements:

  • Integrated data collection systems
  • Regular progression analytics and reporting
  • Dashboards for programme monitoring
  • Staff training in data interpretation

Expected Outcomes: Evidence-based policy and strategy

2. Cross-Departmental Coordination

Objective: Break down silos and increase collaboration

Key Elements:

  • Establishment of progression working groups
  • Cross-departmental staff development
  • Shared goals and metrics
  • Regular communication structures

Expected Outcomes: Coherent institutional approach, amplified intervention impact

3. Stakeholder Engagement

Objective: Build ownership and commitment across the institution

Key Elements:

  • Student voice in planning and evaluation
  • Staff involvement in implementation
  • Employer partnerships for curriculum relevance
  • Transparent communication of progress

Expected Outcomes: Sustained commitment, continuous improvement

Implementation Priorities

Interventions will be prioritised based on:

  • Impact Potential: Expected effect on progression outcomes
  • Feasibility: Realistic implementation within current resources
  • Alignment: Consistency with institutional priorities
  • Sequencing: Logical order for sustainable implementation
