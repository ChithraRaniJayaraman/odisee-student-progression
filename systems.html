Systems Model

The model maps relationships between student readiness, programme design, support systems, staff practices, and institutional decisions.

Core System Domains

Student Domain

Belonging, confidence, wellbeing, study strategies, and workload balance.

Programme Domain

Assessment alignment, curriculum coherence, feedback quality, and progression rules.

Institution Domain

Data infrastructure, cross-department coordination, policy coherence, and resource support.

Feedback Patterns

Reinforcing Loop: Confidence Cycle

Timely support can improve confidence, participation, and performance, which then increases motivation.

Reinforcing Loop: Risk Escalation

Late detection and weak coordination can increase academic pressure and disengagement.

Balancing Loop: Early Alerts

Earlier intervention can prevent risk accumulation and stabilise progression trajectories.

From Model to Action

The intervention design targets high-leverage points identified by this systems analysis.
